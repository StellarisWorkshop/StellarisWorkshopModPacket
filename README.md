Stellaris Workshop
Stellaris Workshop is a deeply performance-optimized GregTech skyblock modpack built on the GregTech Modern Community Pack (GTCPM) template. It combines GregTech with Ex Nihilo, Create, Mekanism, and other tech mods, featuring 220+ multiblock structures and a complete industrial progression from the Steam Age to UHV.

From Coke Oven, Primitive Blast Furnace, Steam Rock Breaker, and Large Steam Tank, to Fission Reactor, Fusion Reactor, Supercritical Phase Shifter, and AE2 systems like ME Controller, ME Drive, ME Pattern Provider, and Quantum Entangloporter — all await you in this skyblock world.

Beyond hardcore tech, the pack also integrates magic mods such as Botania and Mystical Agriculture to assist tech-line resource production. Botania offers Mana Pool, Runic Altar, Alfheim Portal, Gaia Pylon, Pure Daisy, Petal Apothecary, and more, letting you obtain rare materials through nature and mana. Mystical Agriculture brings new possibilities for combining agriculture and industry. These magic mods are not isolated — they are closely interwoven with GregTech production lines, forming a large, self-consistent tech ecosystem.

Features
Pure Factory Simulation
The core experience of Stellaris Workshop is a factory simulation from start to finish. There are no aimless journeys or endless mob grinding — all resources come from production lines you build yourself. The pack provides an Ex Nihilo resource system covering the entire game: from your first bucket of water and tree, to mid-game ores, fluids, and chemical feedstocks, to late-game advanced materials. Everything can be produced automatically within your skyblock base.

To smooth out this system, the pack adds custom content such as GregTech Sieves and Create Sieves. Sieving is no longer just an early-game transition; it is deeply integrated with GregTech and Create processing, forming a complete chain from raw materials to finished products. You can drive sieves with Create, then hand off further processing to GregTech pipes and machines — ultimately achieving endless resources without ever leaving your base.

In late game, Void Miners and the Space Elevator further expand resource acquisition. Void Miners extract massive amounts of materials from the void, while the Space Elevator extends your factory’s reach to higher-tier resource bands, meeting ever-growing material demands. Whether you are expanding huge multiblock arrays or stockpiling for endgame tech, these facilities will be the solid backbone of your industrial empire.

Ultimate Automation Experience
Stellaris Workshop heavily optimizes automation so logistics management never becomes a bottleneck. The pack uses mainstream AE2 addons such as EAEP and AECS, greatly lowering the automation barrier. Channel planning, pattern encoding, and item routing become more intuitive and convenient. You no longer need to debug cables and interfaces repeatedly for a complex crafting chain — logistics flows as naturally as breathing, letting you focus on production line design itself.

The GregTech multiblock versions from GTNA inject even more powerful computation cores into automation. The Large Molecular Assembler, Large ME Drive, and Large ME Crafting Unit provide massive computation resources. The Large Molecular Assembler handles many crafting tasks at once, the Large ME Drive offers astonishing storage capacity, and the Large ME Crafting Unit makes complex automated crafting effortless. They are deeply integrated with GregTech production lines, handling everything from early basic automation to late-game ultimate crafting networks spanning multiple mods.

Rich Mainline Content
Stellaris Workshop integrates a large number of GregTech multiblock structures from addons such as GTNN, GTCA, GTSW, and GTNA, and ports many classic GregTech contents, multiplying the amount of game content. From Steam Age to UHV, every voltage tier has plenty of multiblock goals to build.

From classic Platinum-group and Lanthanide lines to rebalanced Silicon Rock and Sterile Culture Medium lines, you will see old friends and play new content. Familiar elements are rearranged into the skyblock resource cycle — old recipes gain new paths, and old production lines gain new meaning.

From classic Isa, Flotation, Zhuhai Fishery, to more interesting steam multiblocks and Primitive Distillation Tower, every step of development feels different. Steam is no longer just a transition; it is a core power system throughout early and mid-game. The Primitive Distillation Tower lets you feel the precision and depth of GregTech production from the very beginning of petrochemistry.

From classic Compressed Fusion and Space Elevator, to Cyclotron and Water Purification Unit, familiar lines await you with new mechanics. Compressed Fusion tests your coordination of heat and energy, the Space Elevator extends logistics to the sky, and the Cyclotron and Water Purification Unit bring finer chemical and particle processing flows. Every production line is carefully rebalanced and tightly coupled with the skyblock resource system, giving you a constant sense of achievement as you build and debug.

Quality of Life & Guided Progression
Stellaris Workshop includes many practical features originally treated as “private goods,” including wireless power transmission, programmable chests, advanced terminals, giant hatches, and a combined pattern terminal, significantly improving smoothness and convenience.

At the same time, the pack includes a large number of quests, shops, and quest rewards. You can enjoy a laid-back, smooth experience while always having clear goals and generous rewards — never dragged down by tedious operations, and never lost in skyblock industry.
